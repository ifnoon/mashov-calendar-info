Mashov Family Calendar Sync

Privacy policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

This policy covers the Google Calendar connection in the private Mashov family app, operated by Roy Antebi. Contact: royantebi@gmail.com.

Information accessed

When you authorize a connection, Mashov receives your Google account identifier and verified email address, a list of calendars your account can read, and calendar events. Event data can include titles, descriptions, dates and times, locations, recurrence information, update timestamps, identifiers, and links back to Google Calendar. Access is read-only. Mashov does not request your Google password or Gmail messages.

Purpose and use

Calendar events are read to identify the child mentions configured by a parent for that family. Matching events are copied into the corresponding children’s Mashov calendars and kept in sync. The sync covers a rolling window from 30 days in the past to 730 days in the future. Nonmatching event bodies are not saved as calendar entries.

Storage and access

Matching event information, account and calendar identifiers, family and child routing, and connection status are stored in the private Mashov database hosted on Railway. Web-connection refresh tokens are encrypted using AES-256-GCM with a server-held key. Earlier desktop authorizations may instead be kept in private files on the operator’s Mac. Short-lived access tokens are used to call Google APIs.

The operator administers the system. Connected data is available within the assigned family according to the app’s parent and child access controls. Operational logs record sync status and counts; they are not intended to contain tokens or event bodies.

Sharing and service providers

Mashov communicates with Google to obtain authorization and read calendars. Railway provides the application hosting and database infrastructure, and the operator’s Mac runs the sync worker. GitHub Pages hosts these public information pages; your Google tokens and private calendar records are not published here.

Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. It is used to provide the family calendar synchronization described here. Mashov Family Calendar Sync’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Authorization is retained while the connection is active. Disconnecting a web connection in Mashov Settings deletes its stored authorization and stops future imports. Existing imported events remain unless removed by reconciliation or deleted by the operator. Removing a mention or cancelling an event removes the corresponding imported event during a successful sync within the sync window.

To request deletion of your connection, imported event data, or an earlier desktop authorization, email the operator. Revoking Google access stops API access but does not automatically delete information already imported into Mashov.

Your choices

You can decline authorization, disconnect in Mashov Settings, or revoke access at Google Account connections. Parents choose the child mentions and should authorize only accounts and calendars whose information they intend to make available in their family’s Mashov calendars.

Changes

This page will be updated if the connection’s data practices change. Contact the operator with questions or deletion requests.
