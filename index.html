Mashov Family Calendar Sync

Keep children’s calendars up to date with family events from Google Calendar.

Mashov is a private household organizer operated by Roy Antebi for a small group of families known personally to the operator. It is not affiliated with Google or the Mashov school-information service.

How it works

A parent connects a Google account in their family’s settings and chooses each child’s mention, such as @name. An event with that mention in its title or description is copied into that child’s calendar. An event can mention multiple children. Updates, cancelled events, and removed mentions are reconciled during subsequent syncs.

Why we request Google access

  • Read your calendar list: discover the calendars your account can read.
  • Read calendar events: find child mentions in event titles and descriptions and synchronize matching events. Mashov does not create, edit, or delete events in Google Calendar.
  • Basic account identity and email: identify the account you connect and associate it with the correct family.

A sync worker runs on the operator’s Mac every five minutes while awake, between 08:00 and 22:00 Israel time. Google data and matching events are handled by the Mac and the private Mashov server hosted on Railway. Family accounts see their own family’s information.

Connect or disconnect

Parents connect through the Google Calendar section in Mashov Settings. Disconnecting there stops that web connection’s future imports. You can also revoke access through your Google Account’s third-party connections.
